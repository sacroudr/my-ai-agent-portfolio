**Degree:** Master of Science (Diplôme d'Ingénieur)
**Specialization:** Software Engineering / Digital Engineering

### About ISEN Brest
ISEN is one of France's recognized engineering schools (Grande École), part of the ISEN Yncréa group. The diploma is state-recognized at the Master's level (Bac+5), equivalent to an engineering degree.

### Relevant Coursework & Technical Areas

- Software architecture and design patterns
- Database systems (relational + NoSQL)
- Embedded systems and low-level programming (C, STM32)
- Agile methodologies and software project management
- Networks and system administration
- Mobile development (React Native)
- DevOps fundamentals

## Classes Préparatoires aux Grandes Écoles (CPGE) — MPSI/PSI
**Period:** 2020 – 2022
**Institution:** Casablanca, Morocco
**Track:** MPSI (Mathématiques, Physique et Sciences de l'Ingénieur) → PSI (Physique et Sciences de l'Ingénieur)

### What This Means
The French-system CPGE is one of the most rigorous academic programs in the world — two years of intensive study in mathematics, physics, and engineering sciences, designed exclusively to prepare students for competitive entrance exams (concours) into France's top engineering schools (Grandes Écoles).

### Outcome
After two years of prépa in Casablanca, Riad sat the competitive entrance exams and was **admitted to ISEN Brest** — relocating to France in 2022 to begin his engineering degree. This path demonstrates exceptional academic discipline, strong mathematical foundations, and the ability to perform under sustained high-pressure conditions.

### Why It Matters
The prépa background gives Riad strong fundamentals in analytical thinking, problem decomposition, and rigorous reasoning — skills that directly transfer to software architecture and engineering. It is not a typical path; most developers don't have it.
