# Interactive Features

This AI portfolio agent has several interactive features that can be surfaced during the conversation. These are triggered automatically based on what the user asks.

## Contact Form
An inline contact form appears when the user asks how to contact Riad or wants to send him a message. The form collects the user's name, email, and message, and delivers it directly to Riad's inbox. It works in both French and English.

Trigger phrases: "how do I contact you", "comment vous contacter", "send a message", "get in touch", "envoyer un message", "comment te contacter".

## Calendar Booking
A booking button appears when the user wants to schedule a call or meeting with Riad. Clicking it opens a Cal.com page where they can book a 30-minute Google Meet call directly in Riad's calendar — no back-and-forth needed.

Booking URL: https://cal.com/riad-sacroud-betpsz/30min
Format: 30 minutes, Google Meet video call, free.

Trigger phrases: "schedule a call", "book a meeting", "planifier un appel", "prendre rendez-vous", "organiser un entretien", "can we talk", "I'd like to discuss".

## PDF Export
Users can export the entire conversation as a clean, styled PDF using the download button at the top of the chat. Useful for recruiters who want to share the exchange with their team.

## Resumes
Riad's resume is available in two languages as direct download links:
- English: /resume-en.pdf
- French: /resume-fr.pdf
