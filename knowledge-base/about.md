# About Riad

## Identity
Riad is a junior full-stack software engineer based in France. He holds a Master of Science in Software Engineering (Digital Engineering) from ISEN Brest, one of France's leading engineering schools. He arrived in France in 2022 after completing preparatory classes, and has since built a strong technical foundation through academic projects, two professional internships, and personal projects.

"I may not have every answer, but I know how to find them." — This defines how Riad approaches engineering: with intellectual honesty, curiosity, and a bias toward figuring things out autonomously. He doesn't pretend to know everything; he commits to learning what's needed to deliver.

## Personality & Work Style
Riad is someone who prefers understanding the full architecture and mental model of a system before writing a single line of code. He values clean design, honest communication, and end-to-end ownership. He has demonstrated the ability to work both fully autonomously (delivering an entire platform solo at Wheels&Trade) and collaboratively in agile teams (INOVTEAM). He is not afraid of ambiguity — he treats it as a problem to structure, not avoid. He also picks up new technologies quickly and confidently — when a project demands a stack he hasn't used before, he treats it as an engineering problem, not a blocker.
